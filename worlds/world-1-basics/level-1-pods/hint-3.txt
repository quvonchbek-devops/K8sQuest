💡 Yechimga yaqin maslahat:

Xato aniq: `executable file not found` — `nginxzz` degan buyruq yo'q. nginx
image i maxsus buyruqsiz ishlaydi, ya'ni `command` ni olib tashlash kerak.

MUHIM: pod ning `command` maydonini o'zgartirib bo'lmaydi (u immutable —
faqat `image` ni almashtirish mumkin). Shuning uchun pod qayta yaratiladi:

    kubectl delete pod nginx-broken -n k8squest
    kubectl run nginx-broken --image=nginx -n k8squest

Tekshiring — READY ustuni `1/1` bo'lishi kerak:

    kubectl get pod nginx-broken -n k8squest

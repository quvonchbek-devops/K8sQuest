🧭 Guiding hint:

The error shows "ImagePullBackOff" or "ErrImagePull". That means Kubernetes cannot find the container image you asked for.

Check the image name and tag in the pod spec. Does that tag really exist on Docker Hub?

Try: `kubectl get pod web-app -n k8squest -o yaml` and find the `image:` line in the output. (This terminal is kubectl-only — pipes like `| grep` are not available.)

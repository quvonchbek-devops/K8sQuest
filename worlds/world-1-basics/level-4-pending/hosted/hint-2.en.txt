🧭 Guiding hint:

`kubectl apply -f broken.yaml` returns an error like this:

  The Pod "hungry-app" is invalid: spec.containers[0].resources.requests:
  Invalid value: "999": must be less than or equal to cpu limit of 200m

That is the LimitRange. Look at the namespace resource rules:

  kubectl describe limitrange

Note: broken.yaml only has `requests`, no `limits`. When a container declares
no limit, the LimitRange gives it its own "Default Limit" (cpu 200m). And
Kubernetes has a hard rule:

  request  <=  limit

So you asked for 999 CPU while your limit is 200m. That is why it was
rejected.

Look at what the pod is asking for and compare it with the allowed values.

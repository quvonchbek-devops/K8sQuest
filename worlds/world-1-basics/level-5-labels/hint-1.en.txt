🔍 Observation hint:

First check that the pod is running: `kubectl get pod backend-app -n k8squest`

Then check the service: `kubectl get service backend-service -n k8squest`

Finally, check whether the service found any pods: `kubectl get endpoints backend-service -n k8squest`

Do you see any endpoint (IP address)? If not, the service is not wired to the pod.

🧭 Guiding hint:

A Service finds pods through **label selectors**. The Service selector must match the pod's labels.

Check the Service selector:
`kubectl get service __APP__-service -n k8squest -o jsonpath='{.spec.selector}'`

Check the pod labels:
`kubectl get pod __APP__-app -n k8squest --show-labels`

Do the labels match? Find the mismatch!

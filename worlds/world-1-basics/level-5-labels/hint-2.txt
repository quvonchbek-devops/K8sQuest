Service pod larni **label selector** lar orqali topadi. Service ning selector i pod ning label lariga mos kelishi kerak.

Service selector ni tekshiring:
`kubectl get service __APP__-service -n k8squest -o jsonpath='{.spec.selector}'`

Pod label larini tekshiring:
`kubectl get pod __APP__-app -n k8squest --show-labels`

Label lar mos kelyaptimi? Nomuvofiqlikni toping!

💡 Near-solution hint:

The Service selector looks for `app: __APP__`, but the pod is labelled `app: frontend`. They do not match!

Fix the pod label with a single command (no file editing needed):
`kubectl label pod __APP__-app app=__APP__ --overwrite -n k8squest`

Then check:
`kubectl get endpoints __APP__-service -n k8squest`

An IP address should appear as an endpoint!

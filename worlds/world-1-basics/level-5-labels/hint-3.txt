💡 Yechimga yaqin maslahat:

Service selector i `app: __APP__` ni qidiryapti, pod ning label i esa `app: frontend`. Ular mos kelmayapti!

Pod label ini to'g'rilang (bitta buyruq, faylni tahrirlash shart emas):
`kubectl label pod __APP__-app app=__APP__ --overwrite -n k8squest`

Keyin tekshiring:
`kubectl get endpoints __APP__-service -n k8squest`

Endpoint sifatida IP manzil ko'rinishi kerak!

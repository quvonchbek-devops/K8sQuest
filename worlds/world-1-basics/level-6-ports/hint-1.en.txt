🔍 Observation hint:

Check which port the container listens on:
`kubectl get pod web-server -n k8squest -o jsonpath='{.spec.containers[0].ports[0].containerPort}'`

Then which port the Service forwards to:
`kubectl get service web-service -n k8squest -o jsonpath='{.spec.ports[0].targetPort}'`

Do they match?

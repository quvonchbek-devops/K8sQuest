🧭 Guiding hint:

A Service has TWO ports and they mean different things:

  - `port` — the port clients use to reach the Service;
  - `targetPort` — the port INSIDE the pod where traffic finally lands.

`targetPort` must match the container's `containerPort`. Compare them:

    kubectl get service web-service -n k8squest -o jsonpath='{.spec.ports[0]}'
    kubectl get pod web-server -n k8squest -o jsonpath='{.spec.containers[0].ports[0]}'

If they do not match, the Service still "finds" the pod but the connection is
refused. The endpoint list shows it:

    kubectl get endpoints web-service -n k8squest

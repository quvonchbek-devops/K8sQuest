🧭 Yo'naltiruvchi maslahat:

Service da IKKI port bor va ular boshqa-boshqa narsa:

  - `port` — klient service ga ulanadigan port;
  - `targetPort` — pod ICHIDAGI port, trafik oxirida shu yerga boradi.

`targetPort` konteynerning `containerPort` iga mos bo'lishi SHART. Ikkalasini
solishtiring:

    kubectl get service web-service -n k8squest -o jsonpath='{.spec.ports[0]}'
    kubectl get pod web-server -n k8squest -o jsonpath='{.spec.containers[0].ports[0]}'

Mos kelmasa, service pod ni "topadi", lekin ulanish rad etiladi. Endpoint
ro'yxati buni ko'rsatadi:

    kubectl get endpoints web-service -n k8squest

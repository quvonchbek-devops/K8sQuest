🧭 Guiding hint:

The init container tries to resolve the name `backend-service` through DNS and
does not finish UNTIL it finds it. The main container does not start until the
init container finishes — so the pod stays stuck in `Init:0/1`.

Look at what the init container runs, and whether the service exists:

    kubectl get pod web-with-init -n k8squest -o jsonpath='{.spec.initContainers[0].command}'
    kubectl get svc -n k8squest

The second command does NOT list `backend-service` — the init container is
waiting for something that does not exist. Two ways out: create the service it
waits for, or change the command so it can finish.

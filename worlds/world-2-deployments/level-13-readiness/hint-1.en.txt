🔍 Observation hint:

Check the pods:
`kubectl get pods -n k8squest -l app=slow-app`

Watch the READY column. Some pods may show "0/1" even while STATUS is "Running".

Check the Service endpoints:
`kubectl get endpoints slow-app-service -n k8squest`

Are pods that are not ready being added to the Service? That sends traffic to pods that cannot serve it!

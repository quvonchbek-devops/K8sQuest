🧭 Guiding hint:

The problem: the pods are Running, but they need 20 seconds before they can serve traffic (simulated with `sleep 20`).

Without a readiness probe Kubernetes treats "Running = Ready" and adds the pod to the Service endpoints immediately.

That means users hit pods that are still starting → 502 Bad Gateway errors!

Check the Deployment:
`kubectl get deployment slow-startup-app -n k8squest -o jsonpath='{.spec.template.spec.containers[0]}'`

You will see no readiness probe (only a livenessProbe). That is the problem!

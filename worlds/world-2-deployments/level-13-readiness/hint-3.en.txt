💡 Near-solution hint:

A readiness probe is needed so traffic waits until the app is really ready.

This terminal has no file editor (interactive editing does not work either) — add the probe with `kubectl patch`:

    kubectl patch deployment slow-startup-app -n k8squest -p '{"spec":{"template":{"spec":{"containers":[{"name":"app","readinessProbe":{"httpGet":{"path":"/","port":80},"initialDelaySeconds":22,"periodSeconds":5,"successThreshold":1}}]}}}}'

`initialDelaySeconds: 22` — the container sleeps for 20 seconds, plus 2 seconds of margin.

The container name (`app`) MATTERS: the patch merges the list by that name.

The pods are recreated. Check the state (this terminal does not accept `-w` — repeat the command a few times):

    kubectl get pods -n k8squest -l app=slow-app

Now the pods take no traffic until they are really ready!

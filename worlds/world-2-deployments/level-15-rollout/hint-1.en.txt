🔍 Observation hint:

Check the Deployment's rollout strategy:
`kubectl get deployment critical-api -n k8squest -o jsonpath='{.spec.strategy}'`

Look at `maxUnavailable` and `maxSurge`. What do you notice?

Try triggering a rollout (change the image tag):
`kubectl set image deployment/critical-api api=hashicorp/http-echo:latest -n k8squest`

Watch what happens to the pods:
`kubectl get pods -n k8squest -l app=api      # repeat a few times (-w does not work in this terminal)`

Do ALL pods go down at the same time? That means downtime!

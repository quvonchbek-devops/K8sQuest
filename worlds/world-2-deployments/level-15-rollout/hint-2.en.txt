🧭 Guiding hint:

The problem: `maxUnavailable: 100%` and `maxSurge: 0`

That means:
- maxUnavailable: 100% → Kubernetes may take ALL 3 pods down at once (100% of 3 = 3)
- maxSurge: 0 → Kubernetes may not create any extra pod during the rollout

Result: every old pod is stopped first, and only then the new ones start → FULL DOWNTIME!

For zero-downtime deployments you need:
- maxUnavailable < 100% (so some pods always keep running)
- OR maxSurge > 0 (so extra pods are allowed during the switch)

See the strategy for yourself:

    kubectl get deployment critical-api -n k8squest -o jsonpath='{.spec.strategy}'

`maxUnavailable: 100%` means the rollout is allowed to take EVERY pod down at
the same time.

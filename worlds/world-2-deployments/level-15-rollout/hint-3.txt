💡 Yechimga yaqin maslahat:

Nol to'xtashni ta'minlash uchun rollout strategiyasini tuzating.

Bu terminalda fayl tahrirlagich yo'q (interaktiv tahrir ham ishlamaydi: bir martalik ijro, TTY yo'q) — strategiyani `kubectl patch` bilan o'zgartiring:

    kubectl patch deployment critical-api -n k8squest -p '{"spec":{"strategy":{"type":"RollingUpdate","rollingUpdate":{"maxUnavailable":1,"maxSurge":1}}}}'

Bu ta'minlaydi:
- 3 replikada kamida 2 pod doim ishlaydi (3 - 1 = 2)
- Rollout vaqtida 4 tagacha pod bo'lishi mumkin (3 + 1 = 4)
- Nol to'xtash kafolatlangan!

Tekshiring (terminal quvurni — `| grep` — qabul qilmaydi, shuning uchun jsonpath ishlatiladi):

    kubectl get deployment critical-api -n k8squest -o jsonpath='{.spec.strategy}'

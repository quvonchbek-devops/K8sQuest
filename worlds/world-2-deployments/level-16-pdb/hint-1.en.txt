🔍 Observation hint:

Check the PodDisruptionBudget:
`kubectl get pdb -n k8squest`

Look at the ALLOWED column — is it 0?

Describe the PDB:
`kubectl describe pdb db-proxy-pdb -n k8squest`

Check "allowed disruptions" — if it is 0, node drains will fail!

Check the Deployment:
`kubectl get deployment database-proxy -n k8squest`

How many replicas are there? Compare that with the PDB's minAvailable.

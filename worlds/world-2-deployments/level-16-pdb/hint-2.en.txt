🧭 Guiding hint:

The PDB says `minAvailable: 3`, and the Deployment also has 3 replicas.

That means:
- minAvailable: 3 = "3 pods must be available at all times"
- Replicas: 3 = "there are 3 pods in total"
- Result: no pod can ever be evicted (3 - 0 < 3) ❌

This blocks:
- node drains (kubectl drain)
- cluster upgrades
- autoscaler removing nodes
- any voluntary disruption

A PDB must allow at least one eviction!

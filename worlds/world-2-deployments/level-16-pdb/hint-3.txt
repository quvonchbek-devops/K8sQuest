💡 Yechimga yaqin maslahat:

Mavjudlikni saqlab eviction larga ruxsat berish uchun PDB ni tuzating.

Bu terminalda fayl tahrirlagich yo'q (interaktiv tahrir ham ishlamaydi: bir martalik ijro, TTY yo'q) — PDB ni `kubectl patch` bilan o'zgartiring:

    kubectl patch pdb db-proxy-pdb -n k8squest -p '{"spec":{"minAvailable":2}}'

Ya'ni: 2 pod saqlanadi, 1 tasini evict qilishga ruxsat beriladi.

Tekshiring:

    kubectl get pdb db-proxy-pdb -n k8squest

Endi "ALLOWED DISRUPTIONS" 0 o'rniga 1 bo'lishi kerak!

Muqobil yo'l — `minAvailable` ni olib tashlab `maxUnavailable` qo'yish. Bu ikkisi
BIRGA turolmaydi, shuning uchun almashtirish JSON patch bilan qilinadi:

    kubectl patch pdb db-proxy-pdb -n k8squest --type=json -p '[{"op":"remove","path":"/spec/minAvailable"},{"op":"add","path":"/spec/maxUnavailable","value":1}]'

🔍 Observation hint:

Check the Deployments:
`kubectl get deployments -n k8squest`

You should see app-blue and app-green, both with 3/3 replicas.

Check the Service:
`kubectl get service app-service -n k8squest -o jsonpath='{.spec.selector}'`

Which version is it pointed at?

Check the endpoints:
`kubectl get endpoints app-service -n k8squest`

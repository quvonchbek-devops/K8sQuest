🧭 Guiding hint:

Two deployments are running:
- app-blue (the old version)
- app-green (the new version)

The Service selector:
```yaml
selector:
  app: myapp
  version: blue  # this is the problem!
```

This Service sends traffic only to pods matching BOTH labels:
- app=myapp ✅
- version=blue ✅ (matches the app-blue pods)

The app-green pods carry version=green, so they do not match!

Users are still seeing the OLD (blue) version.

See which version the Service currently points at:

    kubectl get service app-service -n k8squest -o jsonpath='{.spec.selector}'
    kubectl get pods -n k8squest --show-labels

Compare the `version` value in the selector with the labels on the pods —
traffic only reaches the ones that match.

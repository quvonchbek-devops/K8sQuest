💡 Near-solution hint:

In a blue-green deployment you switch traffic by updating the Service selector.

This terminal has no file editor (interactive editing does not work either: one-shot execution, no TTY) — switch the selector with `kubectl patch`:

    kubectl patch service app-service -n k8squest -p '{"spec":{"selector":{"app":"myapp","version":"green"}}}'

Writing BOTH selector keys matters: a patch merges the selector map, so giving
only `version` would leave `app` in place — but being explicit shows what you
are connecting to.

Check — see the endpoints move to the green pods:

    kubectl get endpoints app-service -n k8squest
    kubectl get pods -n k8squest -l version=green

💡 Yechimga yaqin maslahat:

Blue-green deployment larda service selector ni yangilab trafik almashtirasiz.

Bu terminalda fayl tahrirlagich yo'q (interaktiv tahrir ham ishlamaydi: bir martalik ijro, TTY yo'q) — selector ni `kubectl patch` bilan almashtiring:

    kubectl patch service app-service -n k8squest -p '{"spec":{"selector":{"app":"myapp","version":"green"}}}'

Selector ning IKKI kalitini ham yozish MUHIM: patch selector xaritasini
qo'shib ketadi, ya'ni faqat `version` ni bersangiz `app` joyida qoladi — lekin
aniq yozish nimaga ulanayotganini ko'rsatadi.

Tekshiring — endpoint lar green pod larga ko'chganini ko'ring:

    kubectl get endpoints app-service -n k8squest
    kubectl get pods -n k8squest -l version=green

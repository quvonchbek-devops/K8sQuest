🔍 Observation hint:

Check the Deployments:
`kubectl get deployments -n k8squest`

Look at the READY column. How many stable pods are there? How many canary pods?

Check the Service endpoints:
`kubectl get endpoints app-service -n k8squest`

Count the IPs — that shows ALL pods receiving traffic (both stable and canary).

The traffic split is decided by the pod COUNT — count the service endpoints
separately for stable and canary:
`kubectl get pods -n k8squest -l app=app,track=stable`
`kubectl get pods -n k8squest -l app=app,track=canary`

(This terminal does not accept shell loops — `for ... done` — or streaming
commands: each line is a separate kubectl call.)

Is the ratio close to 50/50? For a canary that is wrong!

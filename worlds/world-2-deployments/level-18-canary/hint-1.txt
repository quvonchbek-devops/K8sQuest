🔍 Kuzatuv maslahat:

Deployment larni tekshiring:
`kubectl get deployments -n k8squest`

READY ustuniga qarang. Qancha stable pod bor? Qancha canary pod?

Service endpoint larini tekshiring:
`kubectl get endpoints app-service -n k8squest`

IP larni saning — bu trafik qabul qilayotgan BARCHA pod larni ko'rsatadi (ham stable, ham canary).

Trafik taqsimoti pod SONI bilan belgilanadi — service endpoint larini stable va
canary bo'yicha alohida saning:
`kubectl get pods -n k8squest -l app=app,track=stable`
`kubectl get pods -n k8squest -l app=app,track=canary`

(Bu terminal shell tsikli — `for ... done` — va uzluksiz buyruqlarni qabul
qilmaydi: har satr alohida kubectl chaqiruvi.)

Nisbat 50/50 ga yaqinmi? Canary uchun bu noto'g'ri!

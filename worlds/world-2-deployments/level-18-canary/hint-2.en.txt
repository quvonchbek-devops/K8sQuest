🧭 Guiding hint:

In a canary deployment you normally want:
- 90% of traffic → the stable version
- 10% of traffic → the canary version (the new version under test)

Right now:
- app-stable: 5 replicas
- app-canary: 5 replicas
- Total: 10 pods

That gives a 50/50 split (5/10 = 50%)!

With whole numbers, a 10% canary needs:
- 9 stable : 1 canary (9/10 = 90%, 1/10 = 10%)

The problem: too many canary replicas!

Look at the ratio through the pod COUNT — traffic is split in that ratio:

    kubectl get deployment app-stable app-canary -n k8squest
    kubectl get endpoints app-service -n k8squest

The numbers in the READY column are not 9:1 — that is the problem.

💡 Yechimga yaqin maslahat:

90/10 taqsimotga erishish uchun replika sonlarini to'g'rilang.

Qadamlar:
1. Stable ni 9 replikaga scale qiling:
   `kubectl scale deployment app-stable --replicas=9 -n k8squest`

2. Canary ni 1 replikaga scale qiling:
   `kubectl scale deployment app-canary --replicas=1 -n k8squest`

3. Pod lar tayyor bo'lishini kuting:
   `kubectl get deployments -n k8squest      # bir necha marta takrorlang (-w bu terminalda ishlamaydi)`

4. Taqsimotni tekshiring — tayyor pod sonini alohida so'rang:
   `kubectl get deployment app-stable -n k8squest -o jsonpath='{.status.readyReplicas}'`
   `kubectl get deployment app-canary -n k8squest -o jsonpath='{.status.readyReplicas}'`

   (Bu terminal faqat kubectl ni qabul qiladi: shell o'zgaruvchisi, `echo` va
   `$( )` ishlamaydi — foizni o'zingiz hisoblang.)

Birinchisi 9, ikkinchisi 1 qaytarsa taqsimot 90/10 — ya'ni canary 10%.

Trafik ham shu nisbatda ketayotganini endpoint soni bilan ko'rish mumkin:

    kubectl get endpoints app-service -n k8squest

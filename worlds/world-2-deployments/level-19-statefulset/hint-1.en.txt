🔍 Observation hint:

Check the current resource type:
`kubectl get deployments,statefulsets -n k8squest`

Is it a Deployment or a StatefulSet?

Check the pod names:
`kubectl get pods -n k8squest -l app=database`

Deployment pods get random names: database-7d8f9c-abc12
StatefulSet pods get stable names: database-0, database-1, database-2

Stateful applications (databases, message queues) need a stable identity!

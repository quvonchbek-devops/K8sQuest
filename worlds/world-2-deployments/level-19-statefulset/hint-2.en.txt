🧭 Guiding hint:

The problem: a Deployment is used for a database.

Deployments are meant for STATELESS applications (web servers, APIs):
- any pod may be replaced
- pods are interchangeable
- no stable identity needed

Databases are STATEFUL and require:
- stable pod names (database-0, database-1)
- stable network IDs (database-0.database-service)
- persistent storage per pod
- ordered startup (primary before replicas)
- ordered shutdown (replicas before primary)

Solution: use a StatefulSet instead of a Deployment!

Look at the pod NAMES — a Deployment gives them a random suffix:

    kubectl get pods -n k8squest -l app=database
    kubectl get deployment database -n k8squest -o jsonpath='{.spec.template.spec.volumes}'

The names are not stable (`database-7d9f…`) and the volume is not per-pod
either — both are problems for a stateful app.

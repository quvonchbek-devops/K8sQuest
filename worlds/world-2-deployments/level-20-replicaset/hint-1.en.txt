🔍 Observation hint:

Check what resources exist:
`kubectl get deployments,replicasets -n k8squest`

Is there a standalone ReplicaSet (__APP__-rs) with no Deployment?

Try updating the image:
`kubectl set image replicaset/__APP__-rs web=hashicorp/http-echo:latest -n k8squest`

Check the pods:
`kubectl get pods -n k8squest -l app=__APP__`

Did the update work? Answer: the ReplicaSet spec was updated, but the EXISTING pods did not change!

ReplicaSets do not perform rolling updates — you would have to delete pods by hand or create a new ReplicaSet.

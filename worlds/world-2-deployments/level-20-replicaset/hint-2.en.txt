🧭 Guiding hint:

The problem: there is a standalone ReplicaSet.

A ReplicaSet is a LOW-LEVEL resource:
- Keeps the desired number of pods running
- Replaces crashed pods
- But does not handle updates well

A Deployment is a HIGH-LEVEL resource:
- Creates and manages ReplicaSets for you
- Performs rolling updates automatically
- Gives you rollback
- Keeps rollout history

Think of it like this:
- ReplicaSet = "keep 3 pods running" (simple)
- Deployment = "manage versions, updates and rollbacks" (smart)

Almost NEVER create a ReplicaSet directly. Always use a Deployment!

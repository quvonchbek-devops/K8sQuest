💡 Near-solution hint:

Convert the ReplicaSet into a Deployment.

Steps:
1. Delete the standalone ReplicaSet:
   `kubectl delete replicaset __APP__-rs -n k8squest`

2. Create a Deployment named __APP__ instead (__REPLICAS__ replicas):
   `kubectl apply -f solution.yaml`

3. Check:
   `kubectl get deployments,replicasets,pods -n k8squest`

You should see:
- 1 Deployment
- 1 ReplicaSet (created BY the Deployment)
- __REPLICAS__ Pods

The Deployment created and now manages the ReplicaSet for you!

The replica count must match the task:
`kubectl scale deployment __APP__ -n k8squest --replicas=__REPLICAS__`

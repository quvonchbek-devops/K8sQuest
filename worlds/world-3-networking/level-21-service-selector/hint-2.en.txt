🧭 Guiding hint:

The problem is in the Service selector!

The Service looks for pods labelled `app: backend-app`, but the pod has
`app: backend`.

There are two options:
1. change the Service selector to match the pod labels, OR
2. change the pod labels to match the Service selector

Option 1 is easier — just edit the Service!

Compare the selector with the pod labels:

    kubectl get service backend-service -n k8squest -o jsonpath='{.spec.selector}'
    kubectl get pod backend-pod -n k8squest --show-labels

The result of a mismatch is an empty endpoint list:

    kubectl get endpoints backend-service -n k8squest

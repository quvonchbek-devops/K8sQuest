🧭 Yo'naltiruvchi maslahat:

Muammo service selector da!

Service `app: backend-app` label li pod larni qidirmoqda, lekin pod da `app: backend` bor.

Ikki variant bor:
1. Pod label lariga mos kelish uchun service selector ni o'zgartirish, YOKI
2. Service selector ga mos kelish uchun pod label larini o'zgartirish

1-variant osonroq — shunchaki service ni tahrirlang!

Selector va pod label larini solishtiring:

    kubectl get service backend-service -n k8squest -o jsonpath='{.spec.selector}'
    kubectl get pod backend-pod -n k8squest --show-labels

Nomuvofiqlikning natijasi — bo'sh endpoint:

    kubectl get endpoints backend-service -n k8squest

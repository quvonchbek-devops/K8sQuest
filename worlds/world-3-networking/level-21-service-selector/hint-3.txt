✅ Yechim maslahat:

Service selector pod label iga mos kelmayapti: `app: backend-app` bo'lishi kerak `app: backend`.

Bu terminalda fayl tahrirlagich yo'q (interaktiv tahrir ham ishlamaydi: bir martalik ijro, TTY yo'q) — selector ni `kubectl patch` bilan tuzating:

    kubectl patch svc backend-service -n k8squest -p '{"spec":{"selector":{"app":"backend","tier":"api"}}}'

Keyin endpoint lar paydo bo'lganini tekshiring:

    kubectl get endpoints backend-service -n k8squest

Muqobil yo'l (yechim faylini olgan bo'lsangiz):

    kubectl delete svc backend-service -n k8squest
    kubectl apply -f solution.yaml -n k8squest

🧭 Guiding hint:

NodePort services expose pods on a static port on every node.

Three ports are involved:
- **targetPort**: the container port (80)
- **port**: the Service ClusterIP port (80)
- **nodePort**: the external access port (should be 30080)

You need to add `nodePort: 30080` to the Service spec!

Look at the Service port definition:

    kubectl get service web-nodeport -n k8squest -o jsonpath='{.spec.ports[0]}'
    kubectl get service web-nodeport -n k8squest -o jsonpath='{.spec.type}'

If the `nodePort` field is absent, Kubernetes assigns a RANDOM port — so the
address is not predictable.

NodePort ni ANIQ ko'rsatish kerak (tasodifiy port emas): `nodePort: 30080`.
To'g'ri oraliq — 30000-32767.

Bu terminalda fayl tahrirlagich yo'q (interaktiv tahrir ham ishlamaydi: bir martalik ijro, TTY yo'q) — portni `kubectl patch` bilan qo'ying:

    kubectl patch svc web-nodeport -n k8squest -p '{"spec":{"ports":[{"protocol":"TCP","port":80,"targetPort":80,"nodePort":30080}]}}'

Port ro'yxatini TO'LIQ yozish kerak: strategic merge patch ports elementlarini
`port` + `protocol` bo'yicha topadi, yetishmagan maydonlar esa tushib qolardi.

Tekshiring:

    kubectl get svc web-nodeport -n k8squest

Muqobil yo'l (yechim faylini olgan bo'lsangiz):

    kubectl delete svc web-nodeport -n k8squest
    kubectl apply -f solution.yaml -n k8squest

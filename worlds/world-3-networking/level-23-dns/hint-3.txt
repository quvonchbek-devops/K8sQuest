✅ Yechim maslahat:

Pod ning ARGUMENTI ichidagi hostname xato — uni jonli pod da o'zgartirib
bo'lmaydi (`spec.containers[].args` — immutable maydon). Ya'ni pod qayta
yaratiladi:

    kubectl delete pod app-client -n k8squest

Endi to'g'ri hostname bilan qayta yarating. Ikki yo'l bor:

1) `Yechimni ko'rsatish` tugmasini bosgan bo'lsangiz, fayl sessiyangizda turadi:

    kubectl apply -f solution.yaml -n k8squest

2) Yoki `O'z manifestini yozish` panelida quyidagini yozib saqlang (panel faylni
   sessiyangizga qo'yadi, keyin uni o'zingiz qo'llaysiz):

```yaml
apiVersion: v1
kind: Pod
metadata:
  name: app-client
  namespace: k8squest
spec:
  containers:
  - name: client
    image: postgres:13
    command: ["sh", "-c"]
    args:
    - |
      while true; do
        pg_isready -h database-service -p 5432 || echo "Connection failed"
        sleep 10
      done
```

(Bu tsikl KONTEYNER ichida ishlaydi — terminalda emas. Shuning uchun bu yerda
`while`/`echo` bemalol: ularni Kubernetes bajaradi, sandbox terminali emas.)

Yagona o'zgarish — hostname: `database` → `database-service` (service ning
HAQIQIY nomi).

Tekshirish — log da "accepting connections" ko'rinishi kerak:

    kubectl logs app-client -n k8squest

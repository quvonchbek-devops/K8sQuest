🔍 Observation hint:

You get a 404 when visiting http://myapp.local, yet the Ingress and the Service
both exist. The problem is in HOW the Ingress routes traffic.

Check:

1. **The Ingress configuration**
   ```bash
   kubectl describe ingress web-ingress -n k8squest
   ```
   Look at the "Rules" section. Which path is configured?

2. **Path matching**
   A path is configured in the Ingress — but is it the RIGHT path?
   Your app serves content at "/", so what happens if the Ingress is set to "/api"?
   The request does not match the Ingress rule, so you get a 404!

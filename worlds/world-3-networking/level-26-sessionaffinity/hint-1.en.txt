🔍 Observation hint:

Users are being logged out at random. They log in successfully, but on the next
request the authentication is gone.

Check:
1. Look at the client logs: `kubectl logs client -n k8squest`
   Are "Session Pod 1", "Session Pod 2", "Session Pod 3" alternating?

2. Look at the Service configuration: `kubectl describe service session-service -n k8squest`
   If Session Affinity is None, load balancing is RANDOM.

The problem: the user logs in on Pod 1 → the next request goes to Pod 2 → Pod 2
has no session → it looks like a logout!

The fix: "session affinity" (sticky sessions) — the same client is always routed
to the same pod.

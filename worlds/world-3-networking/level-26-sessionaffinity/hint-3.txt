✅ Yechim maslahat:

Service da `sessionAffinity` yo'q — shuning uchun har so'rov boshqa pod ga
tushadi. Bu maydon JONLI service da o'zgartiriladi, ya'ni faylni tahrirlash
kerak emas:

    kubectl patch svc session-service -n k8squest -p '{"spec":{"sessionAffinity":"ClientIP","sessionAffinityConfig":{"clientIP":{"timeoutSeconds":10800}}}}'

Tekshiring:

    kubectl get service session-service -n k8squest -o jsonpath='{.spec.sessionAffinity}'

`ClientIP` qaytishi kerak. Endi klient loglariga qarang — bir necha soniyadan
keyin javoblar BITTA pod dan kelishi kerak:

    kubectl logs client -n k8squest --tail=20

Oldin (tasodifiy): Session Pod 1, Session Pod 3, Session Pod 2...
Keyin (yopishqoq): Session Pod 2, Session Pod 2, Session Pod 2...

Eslatma: yaxshiroq yechim — session ni Redis yoki JWT da saqlash.
sessionAffinity legacy ilovalar uchun vaqtincha chora.

🔍 Observation hint:

The Service exists and the pods are running, but errors appear from time to time
when pods restart.

Check:
1. Service endpoints: `kubectl get endpoints web-service -n k8squest`
2. Pod readiness: `kubectl get pods -n k8squest -l app=web` — look at the READY column
3. Pod events: `kubectl describe pod web-app-1 -n k8squest`
   Are there any "Readiness probe" messages? Or none at all?

Without a readiness probe Kubernetes treats "Running = Ready" and immediately
sends traffic to pods that are still initialising!

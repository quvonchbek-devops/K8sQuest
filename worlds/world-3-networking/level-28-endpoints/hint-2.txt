🧭 Yo'naltiruvchi maslahat:

Pod larda readiness probe lar YETISHMAYAPTI. Ularsiz Kubernetes pod larni service endpoint lariga darhol qo'shadi, hatto ilova trafik xizmat qilishga tayyor bo'lmasa ham.

Readiness probe siz: Pod ishga tushdi → darhol endpoint larga qo'shildi → trafik keldi → ERROR!
Readiness probe bilan: Pod ishga tushdi → probe kutadi → probe muvaffaqiyat → endpoint ga qo'shildi → trafik keldi → OK!

Tuzatish — ikkala pod ga readiness probe qo'shing:
```yaml
readinessProbe:
  httpGet:
    path: /
    port: 8080
  initialDelaySeconds: 5
  periodSeconds: 5
  failureThreshold: 1
```

Endpoint ro'yxati POD HOLATIGA ergashadi — ikkalasini birga ko'ring:

    kubectl get pods -n k8squest -l app=web
    kubectl get endpoints web-service -n k8squest
    kubectl get pod web-app-1 -n k8squest -o jsonpath='{.spec.containers[0].readinessProbe}'

Uchinchi buyruq BO'SH qaytadi — readiness probe yo'q, ya'ni Kubernetes pod ni
"tayyor" deb hisoblaydi va uni endpoint ga qo'shadi.

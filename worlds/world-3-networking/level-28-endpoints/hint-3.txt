✅ Yechim maslahat:

Ikkala pod ga readinessProbe kerak. MUHIM: probe ni ishlab turgan pod ga
qo'shib bo'lmaydi — `spec.containers[].readinessProbe` immutable maydon
(kubectl "may not be changed" xatosini beradi). Ya'ni pod lar QAYTA
yaratiladi.

1. Eski pod larni o'chiring:

    kubectl delete pod web-app-1 web-app-2 -n k8squest

2. Probe bilan qayta yarating. `Yechimni ko'rsatish` tugmasini bosgan
   bo'lsangiz fayl sessiyangizda turadi:

    kubectl apply -f solution.yaml -n k8squest

   Yoki `O'z manifestini yozish` panelida quyidagini saqlab, o'zingiz
   qo'llang (ikkinchi pod uchun nomni va `-text` ni almashtirib takrorlang):

```yaml
apiVersion: v1
kind: Pod
metadata:
  name: web-app-1
  namespace: k8squest
  labels:
    app: web
spec:
  containers:
  - name: app
    image: hashicorp/http-echo:latest
    args: ["-text=Pod 1 ready", "-listen=:8080"]
    ports:
    - containerPort: 8080
    readinessProbe:
      httpGet:
        path: /
        port: 8080
      initialDelaySeconds: 5
      periodSeconds: 5
      failureThreshold: 1
```

3. Pod lar tayyor bo'lishini kuzating (0/1 → 1/1):

    kubectl get pods -n k8squest -l app=web

4. Endpoint lar FAQAT tayyor pod larni ko'rsatishini tekshiring:

    kubectl get endpoints web-service -n k8squest

0/1 → 1/1 o'tishi aynan readiness probe muvaffaqiyat bo'lganda sodir bo'ladi —
va endpoint ga pod SHUNDAN KEYIN qo'shiladi.

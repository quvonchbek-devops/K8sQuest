🔍 Observation hint:

The StatefulSet pods cannot find each other by name.

Check:
1. The pods: `kubectl get pods -n k8squest -l app=web-cluster` (web-0, web-1, web-2)
2. The Service: `kubectl get service web-cluster -n k8squest` — look at CLUSTER-IP
3. DNS: send a lookup into a throwaway pod (hint 3 shows the exact command) for
   `web-0.web-cluster.k8squest.svc.cluster.local`

The problem: a normal ClusterIP service only gives DNS for `web-cluster` → a
random pod. StatefulSets need per-pod DNS: web-0.web-cluster, web-1.web-cluster…
That requires a "headless service" (clusterIP: None)!

🧭 Yo'naltiruvchi maslahat:

Oddiy service (ClusterIP bor):
- web-cluster.ns.svc... → 10.96.100.50 → TASODIFIY pod
- web-0.web-cluster... → ❌ ISHLAMAYDI

Headless service (clusterIP: None):
- web-0.web-cluster.ns.svc... → web-0 pod IP ✅
- web-1.web-cluster.ns.svc... → web-1 pod IP ✅
- web-2.web-cluster.ns.svc... → web-2 pod IP ✅

Tuzatish — service da `clusterIP: None` qo'ying:
```yaml
spec:
  clusterIP: None  # ✅ Headless qiladi
  selector:
    app: web-cluster
```

StatefulSet larga headless service kerak, chunki:
- Barqaror pod nomlari (web-0, web-1)
- Barqaror DNS nomlari (web-0.service-nomi)
- Ma'lumotlar bazasi klasterlari, Kafka, etcd va boshqalar uchun

Service turini ko'ring — headless bo'lishi uchun `clusterIP: None` kerak:

    kubectl get service web-cluster -n k8squest -o jsonpath='{.spec.clusterIP}'
    kubectl get statefulset web -n k8squest -o jsonpath='{.spec.serviceName}'

Birinchi buyruq IP qaytarsa (masalan `10.43.x.x`), service oddiy ClusterIP —
o'shanda per-pod DNS yozuvlari yaratilmaydi.

✅ Maslahat 3: To'liq Yechim

StatefulSet hamma pod uchun BITTA umumiy PVC dan foydalanmoqda. Har pod o'z
diskini olishi uchun `volumeClaimTemplates` kerak — bu maydon mavjud
StatefulSet da o'zgartirilmaydi (immutable), ya'ni resurs qayta yaratiladi.

1. StatefulSet ni o'chiring (pod lar ham ketadi):

    kubectl delete statefulset postgres-cluster -n k8squest

2. Umumiy PVC ni o'chiring — endi u kerak emas:

    kubectl delete pvc database-storage -n k8squest

3. volumeClaimTemplates li variantni qo'llang (`Yechimni ko'rsatish` dan
   keyin fayl sessiyangizda turadi):

    kubectl apply -f solution.yaml -n k8squest

4. Natijani ko'ring — har pod uchun ALOHIDA PVC yaratiladi:

    kubectl get pvc -n k8squest
    kubectl get pods -n k8squest -l app=postgres

Nomlar `database-storage-postgres-cluster-0`, `-1`, `-2` ko'rinishida bo'ladi:
StatefulSet template nomiga pod nomini qo'shib beradi. PVC lar `Bound`,
pod lar tartib bilan (0 → 1 → 2) ko'tarilishi kerak.

Diqqat: `volumeClaimTemplates[].metadata.name` va konteynerdagi
`volumeMounts[].name` MOS bo'lishi shart — aks holda pod volume ni
topolmaydi.

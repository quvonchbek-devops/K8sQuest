✅ Maslahat 3: To'liq Yechim

PVC mavjud bo'lmagan `premium-ssd` sinfini so'ragan, shuning uchun abadiy
`Pending` da. `spec.storageClassName` — immutable maydon, ya'ni PVC ni
tahrirlab bo'lmaydi: uni QAYTA yaratish kerak.

Eng ishonchli yo'l — sinf nomini UMUMAN yozmaslik: o'shanda klasterning
default StorageClass i ishlatiladi (nomi har klasterda boshqacha: k3s da
`local-path`, kind da `standard`, EKS da `gp2`).

1. PVC ni ishlatayotgan pod ni, keyin PVC ning o'zini o'chiring:

    kubectl delete pod data-processor -n k8squest
    kubectl delete pvc app-storage -n k8squest

2. Sinf ko'rsatilmagan variantni qo'llang (`Yechimni ko'rsatish` dan keyin
   fayl sessiyangizda turadi):

    kubectl apply -f solution.yaml -n k8squest

   Yoki `O'z manifestini yozish` panelida:

```yaml
apiVersion: v1
kind: PersistentVolumeClaim
metadata:
  name: app-storage
  namespace: k8squest
spec:
  accessModes: ["ReadWriteOnce"]
  resources:
    requests:
      storage: 1Gi
  # storageClassName ATAYLAB yo'q → klasterning default sinfi ishlatiladi
```

3. Tekshiring:

    kubectl get pvc app-storage -n k8squest
    kubectl get pod data-processor -n k8squest

PVC `Bound`, pod `Running` bo'lishi kerak. Agar PVC hamon `Pending` bo'lsa,
sababni `kubectl describe pvc app-storage -n k8squest` ning `Events:`
qismidan o'qing.

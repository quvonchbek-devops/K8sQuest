✅ Maslahat 3: To'liq Yechim

Konteyner UID 1000 bilan ishlaydi, volume esa boshqa egaga tegishli —
natijada "Permission denied". Yechim: pod darajasida `fsGroup` berish —
Kubernetes volume ni mount qilganda uning guruh egasini shu GID ga
o'zgartiradi.

`securityContext` — pod da immutable maydon, ya'ni pod QAYTA yaratiladi
(PVC o'chirilmaydi: ma'lumot joyida qoladi).

1. Pod ni o'chiring:

    kubectl delete pod writer-app -n k8squest

2. fsGroup li variantni qo'llang (`Yechimni ko'rsatish` dan keyin fayl
   sessiyangizda turadi):

    kubectl apply -f solution.yaml -n k8squest

   Yoki `O'z manifestini yozish` panelida asosiy qismi shunday bo'ladi:

```yaml
spec:
  securityContext:
    fsGroup: 1000          # volume egasi shu guruhga o'tadi
  containers:
  - name: app
    securityContext:
      runAsUser: 1000      # konteyner shu UID bilan ishlaydi
      runAsGroup: 1000     # va shu guruhda — fsGroup bilan mos
```

3. Tekshiring:

    kubectl get pod writer-app -n k8squest -o jsonpath='{.spec.securityContext}'
    kubectl logs writer-app -n k8squest

`fsGroup: 1000` ko'rinishi va loglardan "Permission denied" yo'qolishi kerak.

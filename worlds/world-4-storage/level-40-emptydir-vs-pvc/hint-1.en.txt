🔍 Hint 1: Observe the problem

The database pod loses all its data when it restarts.

Check:
1. `kubectl get pods -n k8squest -o jsonpath='{.items[*].spec.volumes}'`
2. What is the volume type? `emptyDir` or `persistentVolumeClaim`?
3. Restart the pod and check whether the data survived

emptyDir exists for the pod's lifetime — when the pod is deleted, it is GONE!

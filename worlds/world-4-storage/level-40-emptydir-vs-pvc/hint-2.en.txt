🧭 Hint 2: emptyDir vs PVC

`emptyDir` — temporary storage:
- created AND DELETED together with the pod
- pod restart/delete = DATA LOST
- use for: cache, temp files, sharing between containers

`PersistentVolumeClaim` — durable storage:
- exists INDEPENDENTLY of the pod
- the data SURVIVES even if the pod is deleted
- use for: databases, user files

For a database ALWAYS use a PVC!

Look at the volume TYPE — `emptyDir` dies with the pod:

    kubectl get pod data-app -n k8squest -o jsonpath='{.spec.volumes}'
    kubectl get pvc -n k8squest

The first command shows `emptyDir`, the second shows nothing at all — meaning
durable storage was never requested.

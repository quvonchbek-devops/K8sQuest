✅ Maslahat 3: To'liq Yechim

Pod `emptyDir` ishlatmoqda — u pod bilan birga tug'iladi va pod bilan birga
O'LADI. Ma'lumot saqlanishi uchun volume PersistentVolumeClaim bo'lishi
kerak.

`spec.volumes` — pod da immutable maydon, ya'ni pod qayta yaratiladi. Ustiga
PVC ning O'ZI ham yo'q — uni ham yaratish kerak.

1. Eski pod ni o'chiring:

    kubectl delete pod data-app -n k8squest

2. PVC + pod ni birga qo'llang (`Yechimni ko'rsatish` dan keyin fayl
   sessiyangizda turadi):

    kubectl apply -f solution.yaml -n k8squest

   Yoki `O'z manifestini yozish` panelida — asosiy o'zgarish shu:

```yaml
apiVersion: v1
kind: PersistentVolumeClaim
metadata:
  name: app-data
  namespace: k8squest
spec:
  accessModes: ["ReadWriteOnce"]
  resources:
    requests:
      storage: 1Gi
---
# pod ichida:
  volumes:
  - name: data
    persistentVolumeClaim:
      claimName: app-data     # emptyDir: {} O'RNIGA
```

3. Tekshiring:

    kubectl get pvc app-data -n k8squest
    kubectl get pod data-app -n k8squest -o jsonpath='{.spec.volumes}'
    kubectl logs data-app -n k8squest

PVC `Bound`, volume da `persistentVolumeClaim` ko'rinishi kerak.

4. ENDI eng qiziq qadam — saqlanishini O'Z KO'ZINGIZ bilan ko'ring:

    kubectl delete pod data-app -n k8squest
    kubectl apply -f solution.yaml -n k8squest
    kubectl logs data-app -n k8squest

Log da "Found existing data" va oldingi yozuvlar turishi kerak — emptyDir
bilan bu yozuvlar yo'q bo'lardi.

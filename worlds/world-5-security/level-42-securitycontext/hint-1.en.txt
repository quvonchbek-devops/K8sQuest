🔍 Hint 1: Observe and analyse

Check what is happening to the pod:

1. Look at the pod state:
   ```bash
   kubectl get pod web-app -n k8squest
   kubectl describe pod web-app -n k8squest
   ```

2. Read the Events and error messages
3. Check the SecurityContext configuration:
   ```bash
   kubectl get pod web-app -n k8squest -o jsonpath='{.spec.securityContext}{"\n"}{.spec.containers[0].securityContext}'
   ```

Look for: runAsNonRoot, runAsUser, allowPrivilegeEscalation
The pod SecurityContext may be too restrictive.

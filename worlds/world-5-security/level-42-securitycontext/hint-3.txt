✅ Maslahat 3: To'liq Yechim

Pod da `runAsNonRoot: true` bor, lekin `runAsUser` YO'Q — image esa (nginx)
o'zini root bilan ishga tushiradi. Kubernetes bu ziddiyatni ishga tushirmaydi:
konteyner `CreateContainerConfigError` bilan qoladi.

Uch narsa kerak:
- `runAsUser: 1000` — aniq non-root UID
- `allowPrivilegeEscalation: false`
- non-root port da ishlaydigan image (`nginxinc/nginx-unprivileged`, port 8080)
  — oddiy `nginx` non-root holda 80-portga bog'lanolmaydi

`securityContext` va `image` (root ga aloqador qismi) pod da immutable, ya'ni
pod QAYTA yaratiladi.

1. Pod ni o'chiring:

    kubectl delete pod web-app -n k8squest

2. To'g'ri variantni qo'llang (`Yechimni ko'rsatish` dan keyin fayl
   sessiyangizda turadi):

    kubectl apply -f solution.yaml -n k8squest

   `O'z manifestini yozish` paneli uchun asosiy qism:

```yaml
spec:
  containers:
  - name: nginx
    image: nginxinc/nginx-unprivileged:latest
    ports:
    - containerPort: 8080
    securityContext:
      runAsNonRoot: true
      runAsUser: 1000
      allowPrivilegeEscalation: false
      capabilities:
        drop: ["ALL"]
```

3. Tekshiring:

    kubectl get pod web-app -n k8squest
    kubectl get pod web-app -n k8squest -o jsonpath='{.spec.containers[0].securityContext}'

Pod `Running`, securityContext da `runAsUser: 1000` va
`allowPrivilegeEscalation: false` bo'lishi kerak.

Eslatma: `readOnlyRootFilesystem: true` ham foydali qoida, lekin bu levelning
muammosi u emas — uni qo'shsangiz ilovaga yoziladigan joy kerak bo'ladi
(`/tmp` uchun `emptyDir`).

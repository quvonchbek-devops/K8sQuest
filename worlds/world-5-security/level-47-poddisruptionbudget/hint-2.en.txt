🧭 Hint 2: Understanding a PDB

A PodDisruptionBudget guarantees a minimum availability during voluntary
disruptions.

The problem: minAvailable = the replica count → eviction is impossible

Fix: lower minAvailable OR raise the replica count.
For example: 3 replicas, minAvailable: 2 → one eviction allowed.

Look at the PDB and deployment NUMBERS together:

    kubectl get pdb web-pdb -n k8squest
    kubectl get deployment web-app -n k8squest

If the `ALLOWED DISRUPTIONS` column is `0`, no pod is ever evicted — node
maintenance and cluster upgrades are blocked. The selector must match too:

    kubectl get pdb web-pdb -n k8squest -o jsonpath='{.spec.selector}'
    kubectl get pods -n k8squest --show-labels

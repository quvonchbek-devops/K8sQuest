🧭 Maslahat 2: PDB ni Tushunish

PodDisruptionBudget ixtiyoriy buzilishlar vaqtida minimal mavjudlikni ta'minlaydi.

Muammo: minAvailable = replika soni → eviction imkonsiz

Yechim: minAvailable ni kamayting YOKI replika sonini ko'paytiring.
Masalan: 3 replika, minAvailable: 2 → 1 ta eviction ga ruxsat.

PDB va deployment SONLARINI birga ko'ring:

    kubectl get pdb web-pdb -n k8squest
    kubectl get deployment web-app -n k8squest

`ALLOWED DISRUPTIONS` ustuni `0` bo'lsa, hech qanday pod evict qilinmaydi —
node xizmati va klaster yangilanishi to'siladi. Selector ham mos kelishi kerak:

    kubectl get pdb web-pdb -n k8squest -o jsonpath='{.spec.selector}'
    kubectl get pods -n k8squest --show-labels

✅ Maslahat 3: To'liq Yechim

PDB `minAvailable: 3` talab qiladi, Deployment da esa 2 replika bor —
shuning uchun `ALLOWED DISRUPTIONS = 0` va node drain to'siladi.

Ikki yo'ldan birini tanlang. Ikkisi ham JONLI obyektda bajariladi, ya'ni
faylni tahrirlash kerak emas.

Variant 1 — PDB talabini kamaytirish (eng tez):

    kubectl patch pdb web-pdb -n k8squest -p '{"spec":{"minAvailable":1}}'

Variant 2 — replikani ko'paytirib, PDB ni ham mosini qo'yish (production da
to'g'riroq: 3 replikadan 2 tasi doim tirik qoladi):

    kubectl scale deployment web-app --replicas=3 -n k8squest
    kubectl patch pdb web-pdb -n k8squest -p '{"spec":{"minAvailable":2}}'

Tekshiring — `ALLOWED DISRUPTIONS` ustuni 0 dan katta bo'lishi kerak:

    kubectl get pdb web-pdb -n k8squest
    kubectl get deployment web-app -n k8squest

Muqobil shakl — `minAvailable` o'rniga `maxUnavailable` (ikkisi birga
turolmaydi, shuning uchun almashtirish JSON patch bilan):

    kubectl patch pdb web-pdb -n k8squest --type=json -p '[{"op":"remove","path":"/spec/minAvailable"},{"op":"add","path":"/spec/maxUnavailable","value":1}]'
